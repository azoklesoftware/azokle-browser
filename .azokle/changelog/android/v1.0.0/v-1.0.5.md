# Azokle Android Release v1.0.5 (Build 6) Changelog

## 📱 Browser Lifecycle & Floating Window Architecture

### Accidental Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Window Elimination
- **Targeted Lifecycle Handling**: Fixed a critical lifecycle bug in `MainActivity.kt` where `onUserLeaveHint()` indiscriminately invoked `enterPictureInPictureMode()` whenever `uiState.inAppBrowserUrl != null`. Navigating home, switching applications, or closing the app no longer spawns an unwanted floating Picture-in-Picture window overlaying the device launcher or other apps.
- **Explicit Media Fullscreen Model**: Reserved PiP functionality strictly for user-intended, fullscreen video playback rather than generic backgrounding of static browser tabs.
- **Clean Activity Destruction**: Hardened `onDestroy()` and biometric lifecycle listener callbacks to ensure browser sessions, hardware bindings, and window flags are cleanly torn down without orphan overlay artifacts.

## 🔊 Autoplay Permission & Policy Management

### Three-Tier Browser Autoplay Architecture
- **Global & Per-Origin Hierarchy**: Implemented a structured three-tier autoplay policy model:
  $$\text{Global Autoplay Policy} \longrightarrow \text{Per-Origin Setting} \longrightarrow \text{Ephemeral Private/Tor Session} \longrightarrow \text{Media Request Resolution}$$
- **Default Policy Optimization**: Configured global default autoplay policy to `ALLOW` (matching Brave/Firefox Quantum standards), preventing intrusive permission popups on standard page navigations, background video ads, and media previews across modern websites.
- **Dedicated 3-Option Autoplay Permission Sheet**: Redesigned `SitePermissionPromptSheet.kt` to present a clean, purpose-built bottom sheet whenever explicit autoplay authorization is required:
  - **Allow**: Enables autoplay and persists decision for the canonical origin (`https://domain.com`).
  - **Block**: Prevents audible autoplay and saves preference.
  - **Ask Every Time**: Allows media playback for the active session without saving a permanent policy.
- **Site Settings & Global Defaults**: Added `SitePermissionType.AUTOPLAY` controls to `SiteSettingsSheet.kt` for both global defaults and granular per-domain permission editing and resets.
- **Private & Tor Browsing Sandboxing**: Enforced strict ephemeral in-memory storage (`privateMemoryCache`) for all permissions granted during incognito or Tor sessions, preventing permission state from touching persistent disk storage or leaking across sessions.

## 🔒 Automatic Tor & Ephemeral Session Teardown on App Exit

### Zero-Footprint Ephemeral Tab Lifecycle
- **Activity Destruction Purge**: Hardened `MainActivity.onDestroy()` so that whenever `isFinishing` is true (app exited/closed by user):
  - `TabManager.closeEphemeralAndTorTabs()` immediately purges all Tor and private sessions from memory and tears down underlying `GeckoSession` instances.
  - `TorCircuitManager.stopTorService()` safely unbinds the Tor service daemon, cancels active circuit jobs, evicts idle socket pools, and stops background relays.
- **Disk Persistence Isolation**: Enforced that `TabManager.persistTabs()` and `loadPersistedTabs()` strictly discard any Tor or incognito tabs, ensuring 0% persistence and 100% clean state on subsequent app launches.

## 🎬 Advanced Video Picture-in-Picture (PiP) & Background Playback Architecture

### True Android System Picture-in-Picture (PiP)
- **Zero Fake Overlays**: Implemented true Android system Picture-in-Picture window support across Android 8.0 through Android 15+.
- **Auto-PiP on Minimize (Android 12+)**: Utilizes `PictureInPictureParams.Builder.setAutoEnterEnabled(isPlaying && isFullscreen)` to seamlessly transition active fullscreen video into a floating PiP window when the user swipes home or switches apps.
- **Dynamic Aspect Ratio Clamping**: Automatically calculates and applies exact video dimensions (16:9, 4:3, 21:9 anamorphic) safely clamped to Android CDD boundary standards ($1/2.39$ to $2.39$).
- **Interactive PiP Actions**: Embedded custom RemoteActions directly into the PiP window:
  - **Rewind 10s** (`ic_media_rewind_10.xml`)
  - **Play / Pause** toggle (`ic_media_play.xml`, `ic_media_pause.xml`)
  - **Fast Forward 10s** (`ic_media_forward_10.xml`)
- **Minimalist PiP Viewport Rendering**: When entering PiP mode, `BrowserScreen.kt` instantly hides the Omnibar, bottom toolbar, tab switchers, and overlays, ensuring 100% of the floating window surface is dedicated to video playback.

### Background Audio/Video Playback & Lock-Screen MediaSession
- **System MediaSession Integration**: Direct bridge between Mozilla GeckoView Quantum `MediaSession.Delegate` and Android's `MediaSessionCompat` (`MediaPlaybackManager.kt`).
- **Lock-Screen Transport Controls & Artwork**: Live track metadata (title, artist/domain, album, high-resolution artwork) and position state streaming to lock-screen media controls, Bluetooth audio receivers, Android Auto, and WearOS.
- **Foreground Media Service (`MediaPlaybackService.kt`)**: Notification with `NotificationCompat.MediaStyle` maintaining smooth background listening when the screen is locked or the app is minimized (respecting the user's `isBackgroundAudioEnabled` setting).
- **Audio Focus & Noisy Audio Receiver**: Automatically requests `AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN`, handles transient ducking during navigation/calls, and pauses playback immediately upon headphone or Bluetooth disconnection (`ACTION_AUDIO_BECOMING_NOISY`).
- **Clean Lifecycle & Zero Leaks**: Immediate teardown of foreground service, notification, and media session when the active tab is closed, private/Tor session is destroyed, or the app is dismissed.
