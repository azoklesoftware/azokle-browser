# Azokle Android Release v2.0.2 Changelog

## 🛡️ Production Crash Elimination & Native ANR Resolution

---

### 1. 🧅 Tor Daemon JNI `NoSuchFieldError` Elimination (`proguard-rules.pro`)
- **Resolved Fatal JNI Reflection Crash**: Fixed `java.lang.NoSuchFieldError` in `org.torproject.jni.TorService.mainConfigurationFree`.
- **R8 Proguard Rules Hardening**:
  - Identified that R8 release shrinking stripped and obfuscated private/internal C-struct pointers and configuration fields accessed by `libtor.so` via `GetFieldID`.
  - Added full preservation rules for `org.torproject.**`, `info.guardianproject.**`, and `com.msopentech.thali.toronionproxy.**`, ensuring all native JNI method bindings and data structures remain intact in release builds.

---

### 2. ⚡ GeckoView Native Lock Contention & ANR Resolution (`AzokleApplication.kt`)
- **Resolved Input Dispatching Timed Out ANR**: Fixed `mozilla::detail::ConditionVariableImpl::wait_for` native mutex lock contention on the Main UI Thread.
- **Main-Thread Bound Initialization**:
  - Eliminated the detached background thread in `AzokleApplication.onCreate()` that was concurrently instantiating `GeckoRuntime` while `MainActivity` and `GeckoView` were binding sessions on the UI thread.
  - Aligned `GeckoRuntime` instantiation with the Android Main Looper, allowing Gecko's internal multi-threaded engine (`GeckoMain`, `Compositor`, `GPU`) to coordinate native condition variables without deadlocking UI input dispatching.

---

### 3. 🔒 Complete GeckoView JNI Precision Protection
- Hardened `proguard-rules.pro` to keep all classes, methods, and member variables across `org.mozilla.geckoview.**` and `org.mozilla.gecko.**`, preventing native-to-Java callback failures and condition variable stalls.

---

### 4. 🎙️ Graceful Voice Search Availability Detection (GrapheneOS / LineageOS / CalyxOS / Older Devices)
- **Universal Availability Detection (`VoiceSearchHelper.kt`)**: Added dual-layer capability check using both `SpeechRecognizer.isRecognitionAvailable` and `PackageManager.queryIntentActivities(ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH)` with `<queries>` visibility declarations for Android 11+ (API 30+).
- **Dynamic UI Adaptation**:
  - Automatically hides microphone buttons and vertical separators in `SearchBarCapsule`, `BrowserOmnibar`, `SuggestionsSheet`, and `BrowserUrlEditSheet` when running on de-Googled ROMs (GrapheneOS, LineageOS, CalyxOS), AOSP builds without speech engines, or older unsupported devices.
  - Automatically hides voice search triggers in home screen widgets (`AzokleSearchWidgetProvider` & `AzokleTorSearchWidgetProvider`).
  - Added graceful fallback in `MainActivity` for voice intents.

---

### 5. 📷 De-Googled OS & Camera HAL Hardening for QR Code Scanner (`QrCodeImageAnalyzer.kt`)
- **Camera HAL Row-Stride Alignment Fix**:
  - Eliminated sensor frame shearing caused by HAL padding where `plane.rowStride != imageProxy.width` on custom ROMs (GrapheneOS, LineageOS, CalyxOS) and Qualcomm/Tensor camera drivers.
  - Implemented row-by-row, stride-safe tightly packed Y-plane luminance extraction.
- **Dual-Pass Decoding (Normal + Inverted QR Codes)**:
  - Added multi-pass binarization to seamlessly decode standard QR codes as well as inverted/dark-mode QR codes (white-on-black) commonly used in Tor onion links and crypto wallets.
- **Offline Privacy ZXing Engine**:
  - Full local scanning with zero Google Play Services or network telemetry dependencies.

---

### 6. 🌊 Natural Webpage Scroll & In-Page Overlay/Sheet Protection (`GeckoSwipeRefreshLayout.kt` & `NestedGeckoView.kt`)
- **Resolved Accidental Page Reload on Downward Swipes inside Playlists & Sheets**:
  - **Root Cause Identified**: When interacting with YouTube playlist sheets, comments sheets, bottom modals, or overlay drawers, the root window scroll remained at `0`, causing `SwipeRefreshLayout` to mistakenly treat downward swipes inside the sheet as whole-page pull-to-refresh actions.
  - **Touch Origin Clamping (`maxPullDownOriginPx = 180dp`)**: Pull-to-refresh is strictly clamped to gestures originating in the top header/toolbar zone (matching Brave and Firefox).
  - **In-Page Gesture Authority**: Any gesture beginning in the content body or over an overlay sheet (`initialDownY > 180dp`) immediately disallows parent interception via `requestDisallowInterceptTouchEvent(true)`.
  - **Deliberate Pull Pacing**: Configured high-resistance overscroll thresholds (`setDistanceToTriggerSync(130dp)` and `setSlingshotDistance(160dp)`) with strict vertical angle filtering (`dy > dx * 1.85f`).

---

### 7. ⚡ Dynamic History-Driven Inline Domain Auto-suggestion & Auto-fill
- **Privacy-First Sovereign Autocomplete (`DomainAutocompleteHelper.kt`)**:
  - Dynamically computes inline domain completions solely from the user's local browsing history, frecency-scored visited domains, saved bookmarks, and recent searches with zero hardcoded lists or external telemetry.
- **Visual Ghost-Text Suffix Rendering**:
  - Implemented pixel-perfect inline ghost text overlay inside `BasicTextField` across `BrowserUrlEditSheet`, `SuggestionsSheet`, and `SearchBarCapsule`.
  - When typing a domain prefix (e.g. `yo`), dynamically overlays the remaining domain suffix (e.g. `utube.com`) immediately after the cursor.
- **1-Tap Direct Jump Card & Keyboard Action**:
  - Displays a high-fidelity direct domain navigation card at the top of the search suggestions with a 1-tap fill button (`NorthWest` icon) and direct navigation action.
  - Pressing keyboard `Go` / `Search` immediately navigates to the autocompleted domain.

---

### 8. 🎵 Brave-Grade Audio Focus Management & Auto-Play Resumption (`MediaPlaybackManager.kt`)
- **Resolved Media Stoppage on Notification Arrivals**:
  - **Graceful Volume Ducking**: Configured `AUDIOFOCUS_LOSS_TRANSIENT_CAN_DUCK` to maintain continuous media playback while Android's AudioFramework smoothly ducks the volume during incoming notification chimes, SMS, or WhatsApp pings, restoring normal volume immediately after.
  - **Android 8.0+ AudioFocusRequest Precision**: Explicitly configured `.setWillPauseWhenDucked(false)` in `AudioFocusRequest.Builder`.
- **Brave-Grade Auto-Play Recovery for Multi-App Playback**:
  - When another app plays audio/video (YouTube, Spotify, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter, Phone call, Assistant), Azokle immediately pauses media and preserves the active playback intent (`wasPlayingBeforeFocusLoss = true`).
  - As soon as the other app finishes or pauses (or phone call ends), Android issues `AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN`, and Azokle automatically resumes playback seamlessly (unless the user explicitly tapped the Pause button).

---

### 9. 🛡️ Enterprise DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) & Quantum Engine TRR Synchronization
- **GeckoView Engine-Level TRR (Trusted Recursive Resolver) Integration (`GeckoConfigManager.kt`)**:
  - Injected native `network.trr.mode = 2` (DoH first with resilient fallback), `network.trr.uri`, `network.trr.bootstrapAddress`, and `network.trr.clear-cache-on-mode-change = true` directly into `geckoview-config.yaml`.
  - Guarantees all web browsing tabs, AJAX requests, subresource loads, and media streams use encrypted DoH (Cloudflare, Quad9, AdGuard) without falling back to plaintext ISP/OS DNS.
- **Provider-Specific Bootstrap IP Resolution (`DnsOverHttpsManager.kt`)**:
  - Enriched provider configurations with dedicated IPv4 bootstrap addresses (`1.1.1.1` for Cloudflare, `9.9.9.9` for Quad9, `94.140.14.14` for AdGuard) to completely eliminate native bootstrap DNS lookups.
- **Real-Time Preferences Synchronization (`PreferencesManager.kt` & `AzokleApplication.kt`)**:
  - Changing DoH providers in Settings immediately updates `DnsOverHttpsManager.activeProviderId` and regenerates the engine config file on the fly.
  - Initialized during cold start in `AzokleApplication.onCreate()`.
- **Tor Circuit Remote DNS Isolation Verified (`background.js`)**:
  - Verified that all Tor tabs and `.onion` domains enforce `proxyDNS: true` over SOCKS5 proxy, guaranteeing 0% DNS leak risk on onion networks.
