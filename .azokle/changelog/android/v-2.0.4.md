### 1. 💖 Azokle Foundation Support Gateway (`BrowserHomeScreenContent.kt`)
- **Docked "Donate" Action Pill**:
  - Integrated a frosted glassmorphic "Donate" button at the bottom-right corner of the Home Screen, positioned above the bottom navigation bar.
  - Features responsive touch feedback, haptic response, a soft coral heart icon (`Icons.Filled.Favorite`), and crisp drop-shadowed typography for legibility across photographic wallpapers.
